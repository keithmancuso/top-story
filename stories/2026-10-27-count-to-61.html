After tonight

What happens next

  • TUE OCT 27 · 10 PM ISRAEL / 4 PM ET
    Polls close, exit polls air. About 7.3 million Israelis are eligible to vote. There is no general absentee ballot, so they vote in person.
  • BY FRI OCT 30
    Final tally expected. Any party under 3.25% of the vote gets zero seats, which is why parties polling near that line can swing the math.
  • WED NOV 4
    Official results published.
  • THEN
    President Herzog consults party leaders and picks the candidate with the best shot at forming a government. That nominee gets up to 42 days.
  • IF NOBODY GETS 61
    Netanyahu's current government stays on as caretaker, and if every attempt fails, Israel votes again. It held five elections between 2019 and 2022.
Why it matters

The first vote since Oct 7

This is Israel's first election since the Hamas attack of Oct. 7, 2023, and it comes almost eight months into the U.S.-Israeli war on Iran. Netanyahu, Israel's longest-serving prime minister, has Trump's endorsement. Gadi Eisenkot, a former military chief who left Netanyahu's war cabinet in 2024, is promising a state inquiry into the Oct. 7 failure, which Netanyahu has blocked.
