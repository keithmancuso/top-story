California · Thursday, Nov 5, 2026

Gov. Gavin Newsom moves to commute all 548 death sentences

Each would become life in prison without the possibility of parole.

What happens to the 548

166 he can commute on his own. 382 need California Supreme Court approval.

The state constitution requires it for anyone convicted of a felony more than once. Count from Newsom’s office, via AP.

One mark per person on California’s death row. No one is released.

What doesn’t change

The death penalty is still California law.

Prosecutors can still seek it. DPI says only a ballot measure can repeal it.

1978Death penalty reinstated. 13 executions since, the last in 2006.
2012, 2016Voters reject repeal at the ballot box.
2019Newsom halts executions with a moratorium.
Jan 2027Newsom’s final term ends.
