Three days of national mourning

29 to 31 October

Wednesday morning on the high-speed line from Madrid to Seville.

Shortly before 9am, a Renfe AVE ran into the back of an Iryo train stopped on the same track.

At least 69 people were killed. The toll is provisional while victims are identified.

There were 544 people on board. About 50 remain in hospital in Castilla-La Mancha, 15 critical.

Spain’s deadliest rail crash since 2013. The cause is under judicial investigation.
