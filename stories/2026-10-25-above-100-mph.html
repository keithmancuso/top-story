Above 100 mph, no link

CAP ~100 MPH (160 KM/H)UKRAINE’S NEED ~280

From the night of Oct. 13–14, Ukrainian satellite terminals moving faster than ~100 mph lost their link.

Mainly Starshield, the military version of Starlink, which Ukraine’s long-range strike and interceptor drones rely on.

Starlink was not switched off. The speed was reduced.

Volodymyr Zelensky, Oct. 16
Ukraine says its interceptors need a link up to ~280 mph (450 km/h).
