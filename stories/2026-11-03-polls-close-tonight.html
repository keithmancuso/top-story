    Where it could take a while

    GeorgiaIf no candidate tops 50%, the race goes to a runoff in December.
    AlaskaFirst results around 12:45 to 1 a.m. ET. Ranked-choice tabulation, only where no one tops 50%, is Nov 18; certification is targeted for Nov 25.
    MaineThe Senate race uses ranked choice. If a second round is needed, expect roughly 10 days.
    Pennsylvania, California, Washington, OregonMail-heavy counts. Pennsylvania can't open mail ballots until Election Day morning; the West Coast states keep counting for days.
    Also on the ballot

    Ballot measures to watch

    California Prop 40A one-time 5% tax on billionaires living in the state, for health care, food assistance and education. Props 41 and 42 are rival measures.
    MissouriWhether to repeal the abortion-rights amendment voters passed in 2024. Separately, Prop A: Yes keeps the 2025 congressional map, No repeals it. Today's House races use the 2022 map either way.
    Alaska Ballot Measure 2Would repeal top-four primaries and ranked-choice general elections.
    Maryland Question 3Would remove the state constitution's compactness and contiguity rules for congressional districts, giving lawmakers more room to redraw the map.
    Voter IDStricter ID rules on the ballot in California, Arizona, Colorado and Nevada.
